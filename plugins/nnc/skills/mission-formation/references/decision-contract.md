# Human Decision Contract

Decision levels:
- D0 preference: low consequence.
- D1 reversible product choice.
- D2 material architecture, data, cost or production choice.
- D3 authority, external or irreversible consequence; explicit human approval.

For D2/D3 each option must make these understandable:
- what happens;
- what is gained;
- what is given up;
- major risk;
- reversibility;
- data/permission impact;
- recurring cost;
- maintenance burden;
- immediate next action.

Include a first-class "I am not sure — investigate/test first" route when a safe
experiment can reduce uncertainty.

Do not treat Other as the only escape route. Do not auto-answer a D3 decision.
A recommendation must have an evidence-based reason and remains only a
recommendation.
