# Bản đồ sách — lead the whole book (v0.7.3)

The training material of this round is the learner's self-study book, *Tài liệu đào tạo Claude cơ bản* ([sach-tu-hoc.md](sach-tu-hoc.md), sections 1–13 and appendices). Your drives (D1 Việc thật · D2 Làm chủ Claude · D3 Dám giao việc · Kết nối) must **lead the learner through every section** of it, across as many vòng việc and visits as the learner needs. **What** must be covered and **how it is checked** is fixed below; **how and when** you lead each section — order inside a loop, the example, the game, the one-touch, whether it rides on the learner's real job — is your teaching choice (nang-luc-giao-vien.md §5). A section the learner already masters is checked, not re-taught. Extra requests from the learner (a job not in the book) are handled with your normal teacher autonomy; they do not replace a section.

Never read the book aloud; teach by the goal. When the learner is stuck, look up the exact section and figure yourself and **show that place** (redraw it, `podium.point`, the exact step) with the section and figure in a small citation line under the visual — never send the learner to the book to find it ([nghien-cuu-tai-lieu.md](nghien-cuu-tai-lieu.md)).

## 2. Default order across loops (adapt to the learner)

| When | Sections usually covered |
|---|---|
| Vòng việc 1 (buoi-1.md) | 1 · 2 · 3 · 5 (mode chosen in part 0, game later) · 6 · 7 (form) · 8 bài 1 · 9 · 12 (first permission box) |
| Vòng việc 2 (return or *Làm tiếp*) | 8 bài 2 · 7 hỏi vặn · 5 (game) · 12 (Đúng/sai) |
| Vòng việc 3 | 8 bài 3 + one of the "9 việc" · 10 Skill |
| Việc kế tiếp / kèm việc | 4 (own session) · 8 bài 4 · 12 (masking a real file) |
| When a job repeats or the learner wants a tool | 11 ([lo-trinh-cong-cu.md](lo-trinh-cong-cu.md)) |
| Closing the round | 13 · open sections first |

The `vung` branch may pull 10 and 11 into loop 1; a `can-do` learner stays longer on 6–9. Do not skip a section because of the order; do not cram several sections into one reply (one touch at least every 3 minutes still holds).

## 3. Keeping track — `trainer.json` `book`

Write `book` in `.lop-hoc/trainer.json` (trainer only, never shown) with section numbers only:

```json
"book": { "1": "done", "2": "done", "3": "taught", "4": "open", "5": "done", "13": "6/8 · 2026-10-20" }
```

`open` (not yet led) → `taught` (led, check not yet observed) → `done` (the "done when" above was observed; for 4, learner-reported). Update it at every Chốt vòng việc and whenever a check is observed. Never store answers given, file names or private content.

**On every return** (`Mở lớp` with `bai0_done`), after the việc kế tiếp and the 2 recalls: pick the next `open` or `taught` section that fits today's job or energy and lead it in this loop. Say the destination in one line (*"Hôm nay mình thêm phần chế độ quyền trong sách mục 5"*).

**Round complete** — all sections `done`, mục 13 passed, and one việc kế tiếp `REUSED_IN_WORK` or explicitly `NO_OPPORTUNITY`: say it warmly in one line, record `book_complete: "<date>"`, and put it on the result sheet. A learner who stops earlier keeps the place; nothing is lost.

## 4. On the result sheet

Phiếu kết quả (vong-viec.md §3) gets one line: `Sách tự học: đã học mục 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 (8/13) · Tự kiểm tra mục 13: chưa làm` — section numbers and counts only.
